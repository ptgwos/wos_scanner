State 646 — Official Rules

Rules and guidelines agreed upon by all Active Council alliances. R5s are responsible for informing their members.

Active Council Alliances
AIR TBR OSM
1
AIR, TBR, OSM are "Active Council" and agree to make decisions together regarding all state matters.
2
Strongholds will be assigned to the top 3 SvS scoring alliances (Based on 70/30 prep/castle performance) and the 4th stronghold will rotate.
3
All 3 top SvS scoring alliances will also take 3 fortresses. The 4th alliance will take 2 fortresses, and the 5th alliance gets 1 fortress. (Based on 70/30 prep/castle performance. Average of 2 preps prior to start of Fortress season.)
4
Zero Tolerance
NO ONE will solo into Fortresses or Strongholds to steal first rewards.
Breaking this rule will result in an immediate action from Active Council.
5
Prohibited
NO CITY ATTACKS or tile attacks are allowed (unless agreed to by both players) and every state member will make every attempt to avoid 646 players in Arena, truck event, Frost Fire mine, etc.
6
All R5s of 646 are responsible for informing their players of state rules.
7
Zero Tolerance
Hate speech will not be tolerated in 646. Violations should be reported to player's R5 and handled by Active Council.
8
State president will be rotated between Active Council alliances.
Requirements to be President: R4/R5 of Active Council alliance and active/present in State Discord Leadership Chat.
9
Punishment
Active Council will enact a punishment of double the troop losses for city attack violations (minimum of 50k) and double the troop injuries for tile attack violations. Only violations supported by reports will be considered.
Deadline: 72 hours for city attack reports · 24 hours for tile attack reports.
10
Blacklist
Rule breakers will be added to strike list and after 3 strikes, they will be blacklisted from all Active Council alliances.
11
Minister of Interior position is to be used for resources only unless holding for state activities (SvS, FDT, and TAL).
12
State members will not solo castle/turrets from PKB, GYN, or HOS and never when in the top 50 on the castle leaderboard.
