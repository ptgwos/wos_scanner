State 646

State 646 — Official Rules

Rules and guidelines agreed upon by all Active NAP alliances. R5s are responsible for informing their members.

Active NAP Alliances
AIR SFZ OSM TBR FUX ELF
1
AIR, SFZ, OSM, TBR, FUX and ELF are Active NAP and agree to make decisions together regarding all state matters.
2
Strongholds will be assigned to the top 4 SvS scoring alliances, based on a 70/30 prep/castle performance split.
3
All 3 top SvS scoring alliances will take 2 Fortresses. The 4th NAP alliance will take 1, the 5th and 6th will take 2, and the 7th will take the remaining fortress.
4
A State Strike Team will handle rule breaks, comprised of 2 players from each of AIR, SFZ, OSM, TBR, FUX and ELF. All NAP violations must be reported to them. The strike team may escalate decisions to council if needed.
5
Facility/Fortress/Stronghold will not be contained with banners or encampments.
6
NO ONE will solo into Fortresses or Strongholds to steal first rewards.
Breaking this rule will result in an immediate hit from the strike team.
7
NAP alliances will not attack each other on gathering tiles, unless specified in KE rules.
8
All R5s of Active NAP are responsible for informing their players of state rules.
9
Hate speech will not be tolerated in 646. Violations should be reported to the player's R5 and handled by council.
10
Main accounts cannot be moved to other alliances to aid in Facility/Fortress/Stronghold battles.
11
No city attacks are allowed in 646 at any time unless agreed on by attacker and defender, including KE.
Does not include Arena, Frostfire, Truck event, or other events that do not cause troop loss/injury.
12
The State President position will be rotated between Active NAP alliances.
Requirements: active participation in council chats/VC meetings, and participation in at least 2 SvS (prep & castle) in 646 where the alliance placed in the Top 10 for prep phase.
13
Scouting is not a NAP violation unless done in a harassing manner, or after SvS matchmaking has begun until SvS has finished.
14
Strike team will enact double the troop losses for city attack violations (minimum 50k) and double the troop injuries for tile attack violations. Only violations supported by reports will be considered.
Deadline: 72 hours for city attack reports · 24 hours for tile attack reports.
15
Attacks on PKB, GYN, and HOS are ONLY ALLOWED when arranged by R5 in advance.
16
NAP violators will be added to the strike list. After 3 strikes they will be blacklisted from all Active NAP alliances.
17
The Minister of Interior position is to be used for resources only, unless holding for SvS roles.
18
NAP members will not solo castle/turrets without permission from their leadership team.
