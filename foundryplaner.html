Foundry Planner

Assign teams to the 11 objects · saved in this browser

Place players and keep “Show in picture” ticked – the picture appears here.
Tap + to pick a player (the same player may be placed at several objects, but only once per object), tap a name to change it, × removes it. The chain button links objects: they then always carry the same team. “Substitute” is a placeholder without a name (whoever is online steps in), you can add several. The picture uses a fixed layout (Prototype Sites, Repair Facilities, then Boiler Room, Transit Station, Mercenary Camp, Munition Warehouse; Imperial Foundry is not included); untick “Show in picture” to leave an object out. A note under a block appears in the picture when it is filled. The small map (top right of the picture) marks the objects that have a block. “Share map pic” makes a large picture with every team placed at its building (Imperial Foundry only appears there). Each object has a frame colour (linked objects share it); the same colour marks it on the maps. The title field puts a centred headline on top of both pictures. The plan is stored in this browser only.
