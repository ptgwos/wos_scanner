Rally Timer

All rallies hit the target at exactly the same second. Hub

1 · PLAYERS & MARCH TIME

March time in seconds from the player's castle to the target. Saved in this browser.

2 · RALLY LEADERS

Tap the players who start a rally. Rally call time (call → march start): sec

3 · IMPACT TIME

Time of day (your device time). "Immediate": the first leader starts in 15 seconds, everyone else is calculated from that.

4 · START PLAN

Pick "I am" in the table, then arm: beeps at 10, 5, 3, 2, 1 and a long beep + flash at start. Keeps the screen awake. On iPhone the silent switch mutes the beeps, the flash still works. (iOS Safari has no vibration.)
